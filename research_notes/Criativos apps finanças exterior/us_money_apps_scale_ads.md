# Paid ad creatives of high-scale US money apps (Rocket Money, Cleo, Chime, Dave, EarnIn, Brigit, MoneyLion, Klover, Credit Karma, Experian, Kikoff, Empower, Albert, Origin, Bright, Tally), 2024–2026

> **How this was researched (read first):** The research was done on 2026-10-10. Direct fetches of Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center/library.tiktok.com, iSpot.tv, ads.apple.com, ads.tiktok.com, Pearmill, Paintgun, NeoReach, Inc. and others all failed. The sandbox proxy returned 403 or DNS errors for them. **So every finding below comes from search-engine summaries and snippets of those pages, not from reading the pages directly. No ad library was opened live.** This means:
> - I could not verify Meta Ad Library longevity (90+ days), live variant counts or TikTok Top Ads rankings for any brand. Each one is listed as a gap.
> - Quotes are labeled **VERIFIED (snippet)** when the search tool returned the exact text, and **INFERRED** when they are my synthesis.
> - Search snippets can be stale or mis-dated, especially TikTok "discover" pages.

## 1. Which specific ads are running now or have run longest (Meta/TikTok/TV), by brand?

### Takeaway
- **Rocket Money** runs the largest and most systematic creative machine of the group. iSpot shows about 155 TV/CTV creatives, and they sit in named series built on one problem: forgotten subscriptions. Examples are "Forgotten Subscription", "Forgotten Subscription: He Sucks", "The Forgotten Subscription Files", "Dave (money hater)", "Boxes" and "Podcast: Sponsor". On top of that sits a very large podcast and YouTube host-read program.
- **Cleo** runs mostly on creator-led TikTok and meme/static content around a sassy AI persona ("roast me").
- I could not open Meta or TikTok ad-library listings, so "longest-running" can only be inferred from TV airing dates and series persistence.

### Inferences
- **Rocket Money's longest-running angle is "forgotten subscriptions."** It shows up on TV from 2023 to 2025, in multiple named series, in UGC, in creator samples and in parodies. A series this persistent across many variants strongly suggests it is the winning angle (INFERRED; I could not verify Meta longevity).
- **Rocket Money keeps reshooting the same angle in different formats** (INFERRED):
  - "Personified subscription" skits, such as a subscription interrupting your conversation
  - Reverse-psychology characters ("money hater Dave")
  - Clutter metaphors ("Boxes")
  - A podcast-sponsor mimic
- **Cash-advance apps have largely moved from TV (2021–23) to TikTok/Meta creator ads with a hard number in the hook** ("up to $500 in 5 min"). That shift coincides with the FTC/DOJ actions (see section 4) (INFERRED).
- **Cleo's edge is persona-driven creative** (roast me, Chief Spending/Wealthmaxxing Officer). The persona makes each ad self-recognizable, and native meme formats beat polished video for them (Paintgun's statics finding).

### Inferences
- **Channel mix archetypes (INFERRED from the above):**
  - (a) **Rocket Money:** "everywhere" performance-plus-brand. Podcast and YouTube host-reads, national cable/CTV, Meta/TikTok UGC, and Apple Search Ads to capture brand searches.
  - (b) **Cleo:** TikTok-first with creators, meme statics, and an algorithmic campaign type (Smart+).
  - (c) **Chime and Credit Karma:** brand TV/CTV tentpoles with celebrities or choreography.
  - (d) **Cash-advance apps (Dave, Kikoff, EarnIn):** creator/affiliate TikTok and Meta with price or amount hooks.
- **Why Rocket Money works on podcasts:** Host-reads let a trusted voice say "I found $X in subscriptions I forgot about." That is the ideal format for a product whose value needs the user to believe it will find hidden waste.

## 3. Recurring hook formulas, scripts and static ad patterns

### Takeaway
- **Rocket Money formula:** problem personification ("forgotten subscription"), then the app cancels or negotiates it, then a quantified saving.
- **Cash-advance formula:** a hard number plus speed plus "no credit check / no interest" (for example, "Up to $500 in 5 min or less").
- **Cleo formula:** self-deprecating roast or confessional humor in a sassy AI voice, with "type 'roast me'" as the CTA.
- **Credit builders:** price-anchor hook ("$5/month") plus denial pain ("Tired of getting denied?").

## 4. Compliance and disclaimer patterns in finance ads

### Takeaway
- Regulators have directly targeted the "up to $X instantly" hook used by cash-advance apps. Cleo settled with the FTC for $17M (Mar 2025), Brigit for $18M (Oct 2023), and Dave faces an FTC/DOJ suit (filed Nov 2024, ongoing).
- The resulting disclaimer stack:
  - "not a bank / banking services provided by…"
  - eligibility ("qualifying direct deposits")
  - instant-transfer fees
  - state availability
  - "savings not guaranteed / results vary"
  - "subject to approval" and "new members only"

### Cited Findings
- **Cleo FTC settlement ($17M, Mar 27, 2025):**
  - Alleged deceptive claims about advance amounts ("up to $250 to $500," while users were routinely offered under $100) and about speed.
  - Alleged undisclosed same-day fees and a mandatory auto-renewing $5.99 or $14.99 subscription that was hard to cancel (FTC Act §5 and ROSCA).
  - [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/ftc-reaches-17-million-settlement-with-cash-advance-company-cleo-ai); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/03/237827-cleo-ai-to-pay-17m-as-result-of-ftc-lawsuit-charging-firm-deceived-consumers/); [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/online-lender-cleo-ai-to-pay-17-million-to-settle-ftc-charges-032825.html)
- **Brigit FTC settlement ($18M, Oct 2023):**
  - Promises of "instant" advances of up to $250 were alleged to be deceptive, and the $9.99 membership was hard to cancel.
  - More than $17M was refunded in Nov 2024. Brigit disputed the claims.
  - [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/fintech-dave-legal-marketing/); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/industry/finance?page=1)
- **Dave FTC suit (Nov 2024), referred to DOJ:**
  - The amended complaint names CEO Jason Wilk.
  - It alleges Dave falsely advertises instant advances of "up to $500" without hidden fees, plus non-consensual "tips."
  - Dave said it will "vigorously defend." The case is ongoing.
  - [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/ftc-takes-action-against-us-neobank-dave-for-allegedly-using-misleading-marketing-); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/dave-ceo-face-potential-civil-penalties-us-doj)
- **Chime disclaimers:**
  - "Chime® is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by The Bancorp Bank, N.A. or Stride Bank, N.A., Members FDIC."
  - "MyPay™ line of credit provided by… MyPay services provided by Chime Capital, LLC (NMLS 2316451)."
  - Eligibility requires qualifying direct deposits; available only in certain states.
  - Instant fee: "$2 per advance" (App Store) vs. an older "3% ($2 min, $5 max)" (Chime footnote). These conflict, so check the current agreement.
  - [Chime App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id836215269); [Chime footnote](https://www.chime.com/mavs); [CompareAccounts](https://www.compareaccounts.com/checking/bank/chime/)
- **Experian:** "Savings are not guaranteed, and some may not see any savings," next to the $631/yr average claim — [Experian](https://www.experian.com/)
- **Kikoff:**
  - Creator disclosure "#ad" + "Special offer applies to new Kikoff members only. Sign up is subject to approval & identity verification" — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/kikoff-credit-ad-girl)
  - "$5/month for 12 months, for a total of $60" — [Kikoff](https://kikoff.com/blog/how-kikoff-helps-build-credit-with-just-5-per-month)
- **Dave fine print:** TINA says critical details are omitted or buried "in tiny fine print at the end of the ad" — [TINA.org](https://truthinadvertising.org/articles/dave-extracash-advance/)
- **Rocket Money cancellation reality:** Cancellation is a Premium feature in which the team contacts the provider, and "there are some services we cannot cancel" — [Rocket Money Help](https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/934402-how-do-i-cancel-a-subscription-on-rocket-money); [Help: why can't RM cancel this](https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/13908897-why-can-t-rocket-money-cancel-this)
  - INFERRED: ads that say "cancels for you" are probably qualified by Premium and "where available" terms.
- **Creator disclosure pattern:** Paid creator posts use #ad and brand partner tags (#rocketmoneypartner, #getkikoff) — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/rocket-money-influencers)

### Inferences
- After the 2023–25 enforcement, safer cash-advance hooks name the amount as "up to," show the instant-fee option on screen, avoid "instant" or "no fees" unless that is literally true, and disclose any subscription requirement (INFERRED).
- For savings and budgeting apps, the safe pattern is an average-savings claim plus "savings not guaranteed" (Experian). A first-person creator claim like "I saved $X" also needs a "results vary" style disclaimer (INFERRED, based on FTC endorsement-guide norms; not verified in a specific ad here).
