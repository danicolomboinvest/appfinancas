# Paid creative by channel for personal finance and budgeting apps (non-Meta): TikTok, YouTube, podcasts, ASA/ASO, Google, Reddit, Pinterest, CTV/TV — US/UK, 2024–2026

> Method note (2026-10-10): this run could only use web search. Direct fetches were blocked by DNS or proxy errors for apps.apple.com, itunes.apple.com (lookup API), ispot.tv, ads.tiktok.com, sponsorradar.com and signup.rocketmoney.com, so I could not open live App Store listings, iSpot spot pages or TikTok Creative Center myself. Facts below come from search-result snippets of those pages and from third-party trackers. **Verified** means a snippet of a primary page (iSpot, TikTok, App Store, Google Play, a brand landing page) confirms it. **Third-party** means it comes from an aggregator or review site. Anything else is labeled as inferred.

## 1. TikTok: Creative Center Top Ads, Spark Ads, Ad Library — which finance-app ads run and what are their hooks and metrics?

### Takeaway
Cleo is the best-documented budgeting or fintech app on TikTok. It has two official TikTok for Business case studies, a Smart+ campaign that reached more than 16M users, and a roast-mode creative line that is native to TikTok. I could not confirm which finance apps appear in Top Ads, or their CTR and engagement metadata, because Creative Center would not load and the Top Ads data that is indexed does not show spend.

### Cited Findings
- **Cleo case study (SAN measurement):** Cleo moved to TikTok's Self-Attributing Network with AppsFlyer and reported a **34% increase in conversion rate and a 46% reduction in cost per action** (verified, TikTok case study page) — [TikTok for Business: Cleo AI (en-GB)](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB/inspiration/cleo-tiktok-success-story-san)
- **Cleo Smart+ case study:** a Smart+ test **reached more than 16 million users**, using creator content sourced through **TikTok One**, the creator marketplace (verified snippet) — [TikTok for Business: Cleo Smart+](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/cleo-smart-plus-case-study?redirected=1)
- **Cleo organic and ad creative, roast mode (Aug 2023):** quoted copy: "Hurts to get roasted, feels good to use an AI financial assistant that actually knows her sh*t. Type 'roast me' in the Cleo app to try it yourself" with hashtags #roastmode #roastmeCleo #roastmyspending — [TikTok @meetcleo](https://www.tiktok.com/@meetcleo/video/7268012311993158945)
- **Cleo creator partnerships:** Cleo named creator Demarre Johnson its "Chief Wealthmaxxing Officer". Creator posts in 2026 carry #cleopartner and talk about the roast feature. UGC also mentions a cash incentive: "They literally give you $20 free just for signing up" (creator post, not a verified ad) — [search summary of TikTok Discover: Cleo Budgeting App Review](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/cleo-budgeting-app-review)
- **Cleo's market:** Cleo shut down its UK operations in 2020 and now focuses on the US, so its TikTok creative is US-market (third-party) — [Medium review](https://maa1.medium.com/cleo-product-review-1de9cf9dd746)
- **TikTok's own creative pattern for the "App: Internet Finance" category** (from an older Creative Guidance page, about 3 years old): Hook = **product demonstration**; key messages = **step-by-step guides and "oddly satisfying" visuals**; CTA = **value proposition** — [TikTok Creative Guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/tiktok_creative_accelerator/pc/en)
- **How Top Ads works:** it lists only ads that advertisers chose to authorize. It ranks by performance signals, not by spend, and **never shows spend or budget**. Practitioners recommend the 180-day window to find stronger creatives — [Stackmatix guide](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-center-guide); [AdLibrary guide](https://adlibrary.com/posts/tiktok-creative-center-guide)

### Inferences
- For finance apps, TikTok creative that works is (a) a creator or UGC face talking to camera, (b) a screen recording that demos the product, and (c) humor or personality, such as Cleo's roast. Cleo pairs this with Smart+ automation and creator content sourced through TikTok One, which is effectively a Spark Ads-style workflow (inferred from the case studies, which do not say "Spark Ads").
- "Roast my spending" and "AI that knows your money" are hooks native to the platform and could be adapted for any market.

### Gaps
- No verified list of US or UK Top Ads entries for Finance > Financial Services or Apps, and no CTR, 6-second view rate or likes metadata. Creative Center was unreachable and search does not index individual entries.
- No TikTok Ad Library (library.tiktok.com) entries were found. That library mainly covers EU/UK DSA-regulated ads and was not reachable here.
- No TikTok evidence was found for Rocket Money, Monarch, YNAB, Copilot, Emma or Snoop paid campaigns.

## 2. YouTube: pre-roll, Shorts and host-read creator sponsorships

### Takeaway
Rocket Money is the dominant budgeting-app sponsor on YouTube. CreatorDB counts about 1.4K creators, 4.1K sponsored posts and roughly 1.3B views, with channels like MKBHD and Kurzgesagt among them. Monarch is the second-largest: SponsorRadar counts 129 creators and 518+ deals. Monarch's standard creator offer is 50% off the first year through a vanity link or code. Copilot relies mostly on user referral codes rather than paid creator deals. YNAB's reads offer an extended free trial.

### Cited Findings
- **Rocket Money:** CreatorDB tracks about **1.4K sponsored creators, 4.1K sponsored posts, about 1.3B views, and 82 new sponsored posts in the last 30 days**. Named partners include **Marques Brownlee (MKBHD), Kurzgesagt, and Wendigoon (6 sponsored videos)**. The page was about 44 days old when indexed, so roughly Aug 2026 (third-party) — [CreatorDB: Rocket Money](https://creatordb.app/brands/rocketmoney.com)
- Modash lists 10 creators with at least one Rocket Money-sponsored post (Apr 2026, third-party) — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/content-library/brands/rocket-money-examples/influencers)
- Rocket Money runs a formal affiliate program — [Rocket Money Affiliates](https://www.rocketmoney.com/affiliates)
- **Monarch:** SponsorRadar headline: "**Monarch YouTube Sponsorships: 129 Creators, 518+ Deals (2026)**" (third-party; the page itself could not be fetched) — [SponsorRadar: Monarch](https://sponsorradar.com/brands/monarch)
- **Monarch offer structure:** creator reads offer **"50% off your first year"** through a creator-specific vanity URL (example pattern: monarch.com/chgo) or a code (example: **WOI**). Widely shared codes include **MONARCHVIP**: $99.99/yr becomes **$49.99** after a **7-day free trial**, valid only on the annual Core plan bought on the web, not through Apple or Google billing (third-party coupon aggregators) — [WalletHub-like aggregator: The Ways to Wealth](https://www.thewaystowealth.com/monarch-money-pro/); [Wethrift](https://www.wethrift.com/codes/monarchmoney.com); [Wall Street Survivor (code WSS50)](https://www.wallstreetsurvivor.com/monarch-money-discounts-promo-codes/); [Slickdeals (NEWYEAR2025)](https://slickdeals.net/f/18052164-get-50-off-monarch-money-for-first-year-and-7-day-free-trial-49-99)
- **Copilot Money:** no official sponsor code was found. Promotion runs through **user referral codes giving 2 months free** (example X post, Dec 2024: "Use my referral code PBXHQ8 to get 2 months free"). A community code "2MFREE" is reported. Copilot does not publish price-discount codes (third-party) — [X post](https://x.com/joetran316/status/1864426149163553110); [FinCompareLab](https://www.fincomparelab.com/guides/copilot-money-referral-code/)
- **YNAB:** the standard trial is **34 days free** (Google Play listing: "free for 34 days, then monthly/annual subscriptions"). Sponsored host reads extend it to a **3-month free trial, no credit card**, through vanity URLs: **ynab.com/skinny** (The Bossticks) and **YNAB.com/HONEST** (Kristin Cavallari's "Let's Be Honest", 2025) — [Google Play YNAB](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.youneedabudget.evergreen.app&hl=en_US); [The Bossticks](https://thebossticks.com/episodes/ynab/); [Apple Podcasts: Let's Be Honest](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/back-to-reality/id1700725168?i=1000700715107)

### Inferences
- The typical script is: problem hook (forgotten subscriptions, or "where does my money go") → a quick app demo (dashboard, net worth, subscriptions list) → proof claim → offer with vanity URL. Rocket Money's landing-page proof is "canceled over 1 million subscriptions" (see section 3). This is inferred from landing pages and offers, not from verbatim YouTube transcripts.
- Offers split by business model:
  - Monarch, a premium paid-only app, discounts price (50% off year one).
  - YNAB extends the trial length.
  - Copilot uses referral months.
  - Rocket Money, which is freemium, sends traffic to a free signup with Premium upsell.

### Gaps
- No verbatim YouTube host-read transcripts were captured, and no data on YouTube pre-roll or Shorts ads for these brands (Google Ads Transparency Center was not checked or reachable).
- No ThoughtLeaders or SponsorsCount spend estimates were found. No creator counts were found for YNAB, Empower or Cleo on YouTube.

## 3. Podcast sponsorships: shows, scripts, codes, spend

### Takeaway
Rocket Money is one of the heaviest podcast advertisers in this category. Sponsorable counts 9,813 episodes across 764 podcasts, with the most recent seen Sep 2026. Rocket Money uses show-tagged landing pages; one is tagged "crimejunkie". It dropped The Tucker Carlson Show after press scrutiny. YNAB buys 3-month-trial host reads on lifestyle and celebrity shows. No dollar spend figures for these brands could be found in Magellan AI's public top-15 lists.

### Cited Findings
- **Rocket Money scale:** Sponsorable reports **9,813 episodes across 764 podcasts**, last seen **Sep 6, 2026**. Networks include **Dear Media, Audioboom, PodcastOne, Studio71, iHeartPodcasts** and four more. Recent examples: *The Sarah Fraser Show, Circling Back, Fitzdog Radio* (third-party) — [Sponsorable: rocketmoney.com](https://sponsorable.com/sponsors/rocketmoney.com)
- **Rocket Money landing page per show:** a podcast landing URL carries campaign parameters `wpcn=crimejunkie&wpsnetn=podcast`, which points to a *Crime Junkie* buy. The page headline is "Take full control of your subscriptions with Rocket Money", and the page claims Rocket Money has "**canceled over 1 million subscriptions**" for members (verified search snippet of brand page) — [signup.rocketmoney.com/podcast](https://signup.rocketmoney.com/podcast/?wpcn=crimejunkie&wpcid=crimejunkie&wpsnetn=podcast&rebrand=true)
- **Brand-safety episode:** Popular Information reported that Rocket Money was a major sponsor of *The Tucker Carlson Show*, and Rocket Money then confirmed it had **stopped advertising** there. SponsorGap also logs a newsletter placement on Oct 23, 2025 — [Popular Information](https://popular.info/p/update-rocket-money-ends-tucker-carlson); [SponsorGap](https://sponsorgap.com/sponsors/rocketmoney)
- **Rocket Money products that podcast reads push:** subscription cancellation (Premium; concierge cancellation), bill negotiation (fee 35–60% of first-year savings), and **Premium on a "pay what you want" scale of $7–$14/mo** (third-party reviews; help center) — [Rocket Money help](https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/934402-how-do-i-cancel-a-subscription-on-rocket-money); [Clark.com review](https://clark.com/save-money/rocket-money-review/)
- **Rocket Money advertises its podcast presence on TV:** iSpot indexes TV spots titled **"Podcast: Hidden Subscriptions"** and **"Podcast: Sponsor"**. This suggests host-read-style creative is recut for TV and CTV — [iSpot: Podcast: Hidden Subscriptions](https://www.ispot.tv/ad/58Jh/rocket-money-podcast-hidden-subscriptions); [iSpot: Podcast: Sponsor](https://www.ispot.tv/ad/BRHk/rocket-money-podcast-sponsor)
- **YNAB:** host reads with a "3-month free trial, no credit card" offer at ynab.com/[show]. Seen on *Let's Be Honest with Kristin Cavallari* (2025, URL YNAB.com/HONEST) and *The Bossticks* (ynab.com/skinny). YNAB's own podcast promotes the 34-day trial — [Apple Podcasts: Let's Be Honest](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/back-to-reality/id1700725168?i=1000700715107); [The Bossticks](https://thebossticks.com/episodes/ynab/); [YNAB Podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/ie/podcast/the-ynab-podcast/id477248343)
- **Magellan AI context:** the public monthly top-15 lists for 2025 are led by Aura Frames ($7.13M in Dec 2025), Amazon ($7.02M), T-Mobile ($6.81M), and BetterHelp ($5.92M in Nov 2025). "Rocket Companies" (the parent group) appears with its largest spend in the science category in Dec 2025, but **no dollar figure** is given and Rocket Money is not split out. Magellan covers the top 3,000 US podcasts and uses modeled spend — [Barrett Media Dec 2025](https://barrettmedia.com/2026/01/21/top-podcast-advertisers-spent-more-than-75-million-in-december-new-magellan-ai-data-shows/); [Magellan top advertisers](https://www.magellan.ai/research/top-podcast-advertisers)

### Inferences
- The podcast playbook is a vanity URL per show (`rocketmoney.com/[show]`, `ynab.com/[show]`, `monarch.com/[code]`) plus a show-tagged landing page with UTM-like parameters. This allows per-show attribution without promo codes.
- Rocket Money's spread across 764 shows and many genres (true crime, comedy, politics) points to a broad-reach direct-response strategy, not a finance-niche one.

### Gaps
- No Podscribe, Gumball or Magellan per-advertiser spend for Rocket Money, Monarch, YNAB, Copilot, Empower or Cleo. Those dashboards are paywalled.
- Not confirmed: SmartLess or other specific tier-1 shows for Rocket Money (only Crime Junkie is inferred, from URL parameters). No verbatim full host-read script was captured.

## 5. Reddit and Pinterest ads by finance apps

### Takeaway
Evidence of budgeting apps using Reddit or Pinterest ads is thin and anecdotal. Finance and fintech make up about 19% of Reddit ad revenue (vendor estimate). Indie finance apps report weak or mixed Reddit tests. I found no 2024–2026 case study of a budgeting app on Pinterest.

### Cited Findings
- Finance and fintech are about **19% of Reddit ad revenue**, the second-largest vertical. Finance CPC is about **$0.80–2.50** and CPL about **$40–100** (vendor estimates, 2026) — [Stackmatix: Reddit Ads for Finance](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/reddit-ads-for-finance)
- A founder test of a finance planning app at **$50/day for 10 days** produced about 313 Reddit page views, **2 signups and 0 full conversions**, and the first ad was rejected for its "crude nature" (anecdotal, undated) — [Product Hunt: My Financé](https://www.producthunt.com/p/my-finance/i-spent-50-day-on-reddit-ads-for-a-finance-app-10-day-results-and-lessons)
- The indie budgeting app Bills to Budget reported "pretty surprising" results from Reddit ads; no figures were in the snippet — [Matt Corey Substack](https://mattcorey.substack.com/p/rethinking-my-indie-app-advertising)
- Guidance for fintech on Reddit: lead with educational value, because overt promotion gets downvoted — [Wolf Financial](https://wolf.financial/blog/reddit-advertising-fintech-community-strategy)
- eMarketer notes growth in mobile-app advertising on Reddit, driven by niche, high-intent communities — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/reddit-mobile-app-ad-growth-marks-rising-value-of-niche-high-intent-communities)
- Pinterest: the only documented finance case is old. **Bank of America's 2016 "Better Money Habits"** reached about 6M people in under 5 months with 29K+ saves using Promoted Pins — [Digital Training Academy](http://www.digitaltrainingacademy.com/casestudies/2016/06/pinterest_case_study_bank_of_america_engages_millennial_savers.php)

### Inferences
- On Reddit, finance apps probably do best with organic presence plus ads aimed at subreddits like r/personalfinance and r/ynab, carrying educational creative. Organic discussion such as "Mint alternative" threads seems to drive more than paid ads (inferred).
- Pinterest suits budget templates and savings-challenge content (a creator and blogger ecosystem) more than direct app-install ads (inferred).

### Gaps
- No verified Reddit or Pinterest campaigns found for Rocket Money, Monarch, YNAB, Copilot, Cleo, Emma or Snoop.
