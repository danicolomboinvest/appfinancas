## Q1. Which ads have run longest or reached the most people in the EU (DSA reach data)?

### Takeaway
I could not query the library directly, so I have no verified longevity or EU-reach data per ad for these brands. The only third-party DSA-reach analysis I found covers Revolut. Revolut reached about 75M people on Meta in one week with 146 new ads in 20 languages. Two creatives with a German World Cup winner and his wife drove about 8% of that week's reach.

### Cited Findings
- Revolut, per Adsmom's analysis of Meta EU transparency data: in one week, ~75 million people reached on Meta, 146 new ads launched, creative copy in 20 languages. Its two top creatives starred German footballer Mario Götze and his wife and took ~8% of total weekly Meta reach. The exact week/date was not visible in the snippet; the URL suggests Germany targeting. — [Adsmom](https://adsmom.com/blog/revolut-gotze-germany) (snippet via search; page fetch failed)
- How DSA data works (vendor descriptions): every ad shown to EU users is archived for roughly 12 months with reach attached, regardless of topic. Reach counts unique people, not impressions. — [Adnova](https://www.adnova.ai/blogs/find-competitors-meta-adspend); [adlibrary.com](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ad-library-api-limitations)
- Spend estimation heuristic from a vendor: spend ≈ reach × frequency ÷ 1000 × CPM, with frequency assumed (e.g., 1.8). This is a model, not disclosed spend. — [Adnova](https://www.adnova.ai/blogs/find-competitors-meta-adspend)
- Caveat on geo-split: if a brand splits strategy by region, EU data shows only "the European edition". UK ads are not covered by the DSA, so UK-only campaigns (Emma, Snoop, Plum, Chip, Moneybox) may show no reach data unless they also run in the EU. — [Adnova](https://www.adnova.ai/blogs/find-competitors-meta-adspend)
- Trackers list Monzo among fintechs with live Facebook/Instagram ads. Motion's finance library includes Monzo among 23 finance brands with Meta creative breakdowns. No ad content was retrieved. — [AdManage brand-spy](https://admanage.ai/brand-spy/fintech); [Motion library](https://motionapp.com/library/finance)

### Inferences
- Since Brexit, the DSA transparency panel does **not** cover UK-only delivery. For UK budgeting apps, reach data will mostly be missing. Longevity (active since date) is the usable proxy for GB, and EU reach is usable only for DE/FR/ES brands (Finanzguru, Finanzfluss, Bankin', Linxo, Fintonic, bunq, Revolut, Spendee/Wallet if they target the EU). This is inferred from the DSA being EU law and the vendor's geo-split caveat.
- Revolut's pattern (high creative volume, many languages, local celebrity) suggests the top EU reach in this category is dominated by neobanks, not standalone budgeting apps. Inferred; not verified for other brands.

### Gaps
- No ad-level data (ad ID, start date, reach, copy) for Emma, Snoop, Plum, Chip, Moneybox, Finanzguru, Finanzfluss, Fintonic, Bankin', Linxo, Spendee, Wallet, bunq, Lydia/Sumeria, or any indie tracker. Reason: library access blocked. Recommended manual step: query Meta Ad Library with country=DE/FR/ES and the page names, sort by "active since", and open the "EU transparency" panel for reach.
- TikTok Creative Center Top Ads (Finance, UK/EU) and Google Ads Transparency were not checked.

## Q2. Which hooks and angles dominate in UK/EU (cost of living, all your banks in one place, open banking, AI, payday, saving pots, gamified saving)?

### Takeaway
UK brand campaigns from 2023–2025 cluster around three themes: getting finances "back on track" after cost-of-living pressure, saving toward a specific goal through pots or Spaces, and human, relatable money stories. Product positioning for aggregators (Emma, Snoop) centres on "all your accounts in one place", cancelling subscriptions, bill savings, and syncing budgets to payday. Fintech ads overall lean on a small set of proven angles (fee comparisons, speed, security) because of regulation and a sceptical audience.

### Cited Findings
- **Plum, "Life Flows" / "Money Motivated" (Founders Makers):** TV first aired 2 Jan 2023, supported by YouTube and Meta. The positioning was explicitly framed around cost-of-living challenges. Reported reach: 14M impressions on owned channels, 16M traditional, ~44M total including TV. (2023, outside the window but the most recent Plum results data found.) — [Ads of the World](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/new-tv-spot-and-campaign-from-founders-makers-to-help-tackle-cost-of-living-challenges); [Plum blog](https://blog.withplum.com/erin-talks-about-how-plum-can-make-your-life-flow)
- **Plum TV:** "Plum wants to help you get your finances back on track with latest TV campaign." — [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/?p=29187) (headline only; page fetch failed)
- **Plum, "Do It With Plum" (Flying Object, ~27 Nov 2024):** saving-focused, across online video, OOH, digital and print. — [LBB Online](https://lbbonline.com/news/do-it-with-plum) (snippet only)
- **Starling, "The Bank Built for You" (Wonderhood Studios, Jan 2024):** three 30-second spots of relatable human stories. One, "The Dessert", shows a young woman using her "Move out" Starling Saving Space to reach her goal. Saving Spaces are the hero feature. — [Starling press release](https://www.starlingbank.com/news/starling-bank-launches-new-brand-platform-the-bank-built-for-you/); [LBB](https://lbbonline.com/news/starling-bank-manages-money-for-all-in-new-brand-platform)
- **Starling, "No Hidden Figures":** first cinema adverts (date not confirmed from snippet). — [shots](https://magazine.shots.net/news/view/starling-bank-launches-first-cinema-adverts-with-no-hidden-figures-series)
- **Moneybox (WING / Little Dot Studios):** new TV campaign tied to a brand refresh. It premiered Saturday 20 September during ITV's *Win, Win*, with a hero brand film plus three spots. 20 Sept fell on a Saturday in 2025, so this is likely Sept 2025; the year was inferred from the calendar. Earlier: "Moneybox Stories" (20something, Jan 2022, three 30s TVCs) and "Who Calls a Cat Wayne?" — [Little Dot Studios](https://blog.littledotstudios.com/en-gb/news-views/wing-moneybox-tv-campaign); [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/?p=80375); [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/creative-works/20something-who-calls-cat-wayne)
- **Monzo:** "Money Never Felt Like Monzo" (Uncommon, 2024, film + OOH). In 2025 it moved from one big brand burst to a "pulsing" strategy ("Saying less is the magic sauce"). BBH London became the agency in 2025: a "Book of Money" OOH takeover in Dec 2025, then the global platform "Get Busy Living" (summer 2026, UK then Europe, "biggest ever OOH"; sources disagree on July vs Aug 2026). — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/monzo-uncommon-creative-studio-ad-campaign/); [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/monzo-investment-brand-building/); [Ads of the World – Book of Money](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/book-of-money-ooh-takeover); [Ads of the World – Get Busy Living](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/get-busy-living)
- **Snoop positioning:** connects banks and cards in one place, flags bad deals and forgotten subscriptions, sends upcoming-bill alerts. Free, with Snoop Plus at £4.99/mo or £39.99/yr. Acquired by Vanquis in July 2023. Launched an Easy Access Savings account in 2025 (from £1). The Open Banking case study is dated 10 July 2025. — [Open Banking UK](https://www.openbanking.org.uk/insights/snoop-budgeting-app-and-savings-account-aim-to-help-build-better-savings-habits/); [Forbes Advisor UK](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/uk/banking/snoop-app-review/); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/vanquis-banking-group-hopes-snoop-deal-gives-competitive-edge-with-budget-conscious-uk-customers)
- **Vanquis "the bank that's got your back"** (developed in-house, 1,000+ bus backs and train-station ads; the parent group's customers include Snoop users). I found no Snoop-specific TV campaign. Searches for "Snoop TV ad" returned only Snoop Dogg / MoneySupermarket. — [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/vanquis-underscore-difference-new-line/)
- **Emma positioning:** connects bank accounts and crypto wallets in one dashboard, tracks budgets, cancels wasteful subscriptions, and syncs to payday. — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/fintech-innovation/money-management-app-emma-bags-2-5m-in-seed-funding). Emma now brands itself an "AI Financial Executive Assistant". — [SeedLegals](https://seedlegals.com/customers/meet-emma-your-ai-financial-executive-assistant/)
- **Fintech ad angles in general:** regulated claims, mandatory disclosures and a sceptical audience push fintech brands toward fee comparisons, speed and security. — [AdManage brand-spy](https://admanage.ai/brand-spy/fintech) (snippet)
- **Revolut EU:** local celebrity creator content (Götze) in localized languages. — [Adsmom](https://adsmom.com/blog/revolut-gotze-germany)

### Inferences
- In UK brand-led work (Plum, Starling, Moneybox, Monzo), the core story is "goal-based saving in pots/Spaces + feel good about money", told through relatable human stories. Aggressive "save £X" claims are uncommon. This is inferred from campaign descriptions, which may reflect FCA financial-promotion constraints.
- For aggregator apps (Emma, Snoop), likely paid-social angles are "all your accounts in one place", "find forgotten subscriptions", "payday budgeting" and, more recently, "AI assistant". This is inferred from product positioning and the "AI Financial Executive Assistant" rebrand, **not** verified from ad copy.

### Gaps
- No verbatim paid-social ad copy found for Emma, Snoop, Chip, Moneyhub, Finanzguru, Finanzfluss, Bankin', Linxo, Fintonic, bunq or Lydia/Sumeria.
- No Chip, Moneyhub or Yolt campaign coverage surfaced in 2024–2026.
- Gamified-savings creative (e.g., Chip prize draws) was not verified.

## Q3. What static formats are used, and how do indie or low-budget apps advertise?

### Takeaway
I found no verified catalog of static ad formats for the named brands. Evidence on indie app advertising is mostly vendor- or tool-sourced, so it should be treated as directional. It points to three cheap, native-looking formats on TikTok/Meta: automated TikTok slideshows, screen-recording ads, and AI-generated UGC variants for hook testing.

### Cited Findings
- **TikTok slideshows:** a developer reportedly took a vibe-coded iOS app to 100+ downloads with automated TikTok slideshows (an AI agent, Postiz for scheduling, a cheap Android phone for posting). Some accounts reached "millions of views". This is a vendor claim. — [Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/automate-tiktok-slideshow-marketing-ai-agents-postiz)
- **Screen-recording ad:** a developer's simple screen-recording TikTok ad reportedly scaled to ~$20k/month in spend at ~25% profit margin. The source is promotional. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/15k-on-tiktok-the-ad-blueprint)
- **AI UGC:** each variant reportedly costs $1–5, versus $150–500 per real creator video (vendor figures). In a solo-founder example (Streaky), the weekly loop is to write hooks, generate ~10 AI UGC variants, then test via TikTok Spark Ads. — [videoai.me](https://videoai.me/blog/ai-ugc-playbook-for-mobile-apps-2026); [ugcad.ai](https://ugcad.ai/use-cases/tiktok-ugc-ads/)
- **Low-CPI UGC:** a guide claims ~$0.30 CPI is achievable with UGC on TikTok/Meta (vendor claim). — [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/scaling-ugc-mobile-app-cpi-strategy)
- **Finance on TikTok:** an agency case study (Mogo.ca budgeting app) cites that 54% of people are more likely to turn to TikTok for finance content (vendor stat). — via [inBeat](https://inbeat.agency/blog/tiktok-ugc)
- **Emma:** reached profitability "thanks to a paywall and social ads" (podcast title, Fintech Growth Insider). Its job ads seek a growth/performance marketer to scale Meta and TikTok paid social with a focus on creative performance. — [iHeart podcast](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1333-fintech-growth-insider-317963765/episode/edoardo-moreni-reveals-how-budgeting-app-338028008/); [Escape the City job ad](https://www.escapethecity.org/opportunity/55816-senior-performance-marketing-manager-paid-social-at-emma-)

### Inferences
- Static formats likely used by aggregator apps include app-UI screenshot cards ("all your accounts in one place" dashboard), subscription-list or "you're paying for X subscriptions" cards, and comparison or feature-list cards. These are **inferred from category norms**, not verified in the libraries.
- Indie global trackers (Money Manager by Realbyte, Money Lover, Monefy, 1Money, Bluecoins, Cashew, Toshl, etc.) probably rely mainly on ASO and Apple Search Ads plus organic or low-cost TikTok. I found **no evidence** of their paid-social activity; this is inferred.

### Gaps
- No verified data on Apple Search Ads use by any named indie app.
- No data for Money Lover, MoneyWiz, Monefy, 1Money, Buddy, Fudget, Bluecoins, Toshl, Cashew, Spendee or Wallet by BudgetBakers ads.
- No Sensor Tower or AppMagic numbers were retrieved.

## Q4. How does creative style differ from the US, and what case studies exist (Emma, Snoop TV, Plum TikTok, Finanzguru TV + influencer)?

### Takeaway
Verified case material is thin. Emma's story is the clearest: early growth was organic (100k downloads in 12 months at zero spend), and the later shift to paywall-from-onboarding plus paid social made it profitable, according to the founder. Finanzguru relies on affiliate/influencer partnerships and radio + social deals with discount codes. I found no Finanzguru TV campaign for 2024–2025, no Snoop TV campaign, and no Plum TikTok case study.

### Cited Findings
- **Emma growth:** 100,000 downloads in the first 12 months with zero marketing spend. — [iHeart podcast](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1333-fintech-growth-insider-317963765/episode/edoardo-moreni-reveals-how-budgeting-app-338028008/)
- **Emma early channels:** about 200k users and the "highest engagement and retention" Connect Ventures had seen in the category (2020). A pitch deck teardown lists word of mouth, in-app referrals, SEO and press as early channels. — [TechCrunch 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/03/05/emma-seed/); [startupfundraising.com](https://startupfundraising.com/library/articles/emma-pitch-deck-teardown)
- **Moreni on LinkedIn (~mid-2026, self-reported, unverified):** of ~350 features shipped in ~6 years, about 2 moved retention and none moved growth. What worked was charging from onboarding, accepting free-plan friction, and spending on distribution. He says roughly half of users pay and the company is profitable. — [LinkedIn, Edoardo Moreni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/edoardomoreni/)
- **Finanzguru affiliates/influencers:** partners need at least 1,000 followers, organically grown communities, a registered business (Gewerbe), and topics that fit the app. — [Finanzguru affiliate page](https://finanzguru.de/affiliate)
- **Finanzguru radio + social:** the "FFH Deal-Fluencer" campaign with HIT RADIO FFH used recommendation/storytelling formats and individual discount codes to measure direct results and drive web traffic. The date was not confirmed from the snippet. — [FFH Mediengruppe](https://www.ffh-mediengruppe.de/werben/blog/442783-finanzgurus-erfolg-mit-radio-social-media.html)
- **Snoop:** no TV campaign found. The search confusion came from Snoop Dogg's MoneySupermarket ad. — [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/snoop-dogg-encourages-people-to-feel-epic-in-moneysupermarket-ad/)
- **Fintonic (Spain):** the only campaign found is the 2013 "Fintonic Gym" pop-up by La Despensa (16 interviews, 300+ media clips, ~84M estimated audience). Nothing found for 2024–2026. Nothing found for Bankin' or Linxo campaigns either. — [Cannes Lions / Love the Work](https://www.lovethework.com/work-awards/entries/fintonic-gym-182568)
- **UK finance TV context:** 2025 US financial services TV ad volume was up 11% YoY (EDO; US data, included as contrast). — [EDO](https://www.edo.com/resources/2025-financial-services-tv-ads)

### Inferences
- **UK/EU vs US (inferred, low confidence):** UK brand work leans on understated, relatable storytelling and regulatory-safe claims such as Spaces/pots, "feel good about money" and "back on track". It runs on TV/OOH/cinema plus Meta/YouTube. Revolut's EU approach adds heavy localization (20 languages) and local celebrities. US budgeting-app ads (covered by other researchers) are generally perceived as more direct-response and UGC-heavy. I found no source that directly compares UK/EU and US creative style.
- **LatAm Spanish-speaking (Fintonic MX, etc.):** no 2024–2026 ad evidence found.

### Gaps
- No Plum TikTok case study, no Finanzguru TV spot, and no Finanzfluss ad data were found.
- No Bankin', Linxo or Lydia/Sumeria campaign coverage was found.
- No direct UK/EU-vs-US creative comparison source exists in what I retrieved.
